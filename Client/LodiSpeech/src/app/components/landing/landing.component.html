Hi, I'm Lodi Thebian.

Licensed Speech and Language Therapist

I’m Lodi Thebian, a licensed Speech and Language Therapist working with both children and adults across a range of communication and learning needs.

My clinical experience includes speech and language assessment and intervention, learning and literacy difficulties, fluency, social communication, voice, and feeding and swallowing.

I have had the opportunity to work across clinical, educational, and community settings, which has shaped my understanding of the different factors that can influence an individual’s communication and participation.

I take an individualized approach to therapy, adapting assessment and intervention to each person’s needs, abilities, and goals.

I also value working closely with families and other professionals to ensure that therapy is meaningful, consistent, and connected to the individual’s everyday life.
